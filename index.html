      About this walk

      Draft copy

      Healthcare work can be deeply meaningful, but it can also take a toll on our physical and emotional well-being. Join us for a free monthly walking group created especially for healthcare workers to connect with colleagues, share experiences, and nurture their own resilience.

      Where to meet

      Lake Murray, by the ranger station Meet at the side of the main parking lot at the park entrance, in front of the ranger station. Enter from Kiowa Drive. Lake Murray Recreation Area, 5650 Kiowa Dr, La Mesa, CA 91942

        Priya is a dual board-certified psychiatric mental health and family nurse practitioner. After years in a busy community clinic, she experienced burnout firsthand and found her way back through coaching, meditation and yoga.

        Today she's a transformative coach who helps people reconnect with their inner wisdom and move through stress, burnout and life transitions. She started Resilient Healers so healthcare workers have a place to walk, connect with colleagues and recharge together.
