        For walk leaders

        Lead a walk

        Walk leaders host a free, small-group walk where healthcare workers can step away from work, talk with colleagues who understand, and recharge. You pick the place, lead the walk, and guide the conversation. We handle the walk page, sign-ups, confirmations and the waitlist.

        Apply to lead a walkI'm already a walk leader

        What a walk looks like

        • Who comesHealthcare workers, and the healthcare-worker friends they bring
        • Group sizeYou choose, usually up to 20. When it's full, new sign-ups join a waitlist.
        • How oftenOne time, or once a month on a set day
        • How longAbout 90 minutes, including gathering at the start
        • CostFree for everyone who walks
        • PaceConversational. Offer a shorter turnaround for anyone who needs it.

        How it works

        1. Apply once. Tell us about yourself and propose your first walk, all in one form.
        2. We review it. Resilient Healers staff approve it, or ask for changes with a note. You can fix and resubmit.
        3. Your walk goes live. It appears on the map with its own page, live spot count and sign-ups.
        4. Use your walk leader page. A private link we email you shows who's coming and their accommodation needs. From there you can propose more walks without filling in your profile again.

        Choosing your meeting spot

        The most common question from walkers is "where exactly do we meet?" A good meeting spot has:

        • An exact pin, not just the park's address
        • A landmark people can't miss, like a ranger station, a sign or a park entrance
        • A short description of how to find you after parking
        • Parking details, including any fee

        Before each walk

        • Check your walk leader page for sign-ups and accommodation notes
        • Decide where people who need a shorter route can turn back
        • Check the meeting spot still matches the pin and description
        • Arrive a little early so first-timers can find you
